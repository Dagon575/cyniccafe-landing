What I run at home

This site fronts a personal homelab: a mix of self-hosted tools, small services, and experiments that move around as ideas change.

  • Search & discovery — self-hosted search and content discovery for the things I actually care about.
  • Feeds & reading — RSS and read-it-later tools to keep noisy timelines at arm's length.
  • Dashboards — a private launchpad for internal services, only reachable over VPN from trusted devices.
  • Automation & monitoring — uptime checks, metrics, and small scripts that keep the stack from catching fire.
